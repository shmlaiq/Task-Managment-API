**Subject:** Pre-submission inquiry: manuscript over 20 pages — "Law, Policy, and Governance of the AI Ecosystem for Human Control: The Human-Centric AI Governance and Continuous Oversight Framework (HACOF)"

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

In line with the IEEE Access submission guidelines for manuscripts over 20 pages, I am writing to request permission to submit the above manuscript at its full length of 33 pages in the IEEE Access template, including approximately 4 pages of references (79 entries).

The manuscript proposes HACOF, a policy-oriented reference model for runtime oversight of AI agents under legal authority, and maps it onto four pillars of state governance capacity, with Pakistan's 2025 National AI Policy as a case study. The length reflects three components that depend on each other and that reviewers would need together to assess the contribution:

1. a complete specification of the framework: its layers, enforcement planes, seven supervisory-agent roles, the governance regime for the supervisors themselves, and a worked incident traced through the full lifecycle;
2. a cell-by-cell comparison with twelve prior frameworks and instruments, together with the comparative capacity analysis of the United States, European Union, China, and Pakistan; and
3. an evaluation protocol, a working prototype of the enforcement plane, and simulation results.

The prototype code and simulation outputs are already separated into supplementary material. Splitting the remaining text into two papers would separate the framework from the capacity analysis that motivates it.

The manuscript has not been published or submitted elsewhere. Use of AI assistance is disclosed in the Acknowledgments, in accordance with IEEE policy.
